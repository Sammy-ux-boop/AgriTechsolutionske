ABOUT AGRIMAP

Where Agriculture Meets Geospatial Intelligence

AgriMap Smart Solutions is an agritech and geospatial intelligence company focused on helping people make better agricultural decisions.

We combine GIS, satellite imagery, remote sensing, spatial analysis, agricultural data and technology to understand the relationship between land, water, crops, infrastructure and markets.

From selecting agricultural land to planning farms, monitoring crops and analyzing routes to market, our goal is simple: turn data into practical agricultural decisions.

02 / 04

Farm Intelligence

Turn farm data, satellite imagery and field information into practical management insights.

Farm Mapping

Map farm boundaries, fields, infrastructure, water points, roads and other agricultural features.

Satellite Farm Monitoring

Monitor vegetation, crop development, land-cover changes and potential crop stress using satellite imagery.

Drone Crop Assessment

Obtain detailed field-level information for crop assessment and targeted agricultural inspection.

Precision Agriculture

Identify spatial variability and develop management zones for targeted agricultural interventions.

Precision Fertilization

Support targeted fertilizer planning using soil, crop and spatial variability information.

Agricultural Data Platforms

Bring together farm, soil, water, crop, satellite, road and market information into useful systems.

03 / 04

Water Intelligence

Understand water resources and their potential role in agricultural development.

Borehole Site Assessment

Analyze spatial, terrain, geological and environmental information to identify areas for further investigation.

Water Source Mapping

Map rivers, streams, reservoirs, dams and other water resources relevant to agriculture.

Irrigation Planning

Analyze terrain, elevation, water sources and farm boundaries to support irrigation planning.

Reservoir & Dam Suitability

Assess terrain, drainage, catchments and spatial factors for potential water storage locations.

Watershed & Catchment Analysis

Understand drainage networks, catchments, terrain and water-flow patterns.

Flood Risk Mapping

Identify areas potentially exposed to flooding and other water-related hazards.

Least-Cost Routing

Collection Point Planning

Aggregation Center Analysis

Farm Accessibility Analysis

Farm Access Planning

Plan practical access routes to improve movement within and around agricultural land.
